I’m Raman Gupta, a video editor, graphic designer and YouTube content creator. I enjoy combining storytelling, pacing, typography, colour and motion to make digital content more engaging and clear.

With over six years of YouTube experience, more than two years in thumbnail design and one year of professional video-editing experience, I bring both creator perspective and design thinking to each project.
